⚠️ Evaluation Limitations & Dataset Realities

Transparent methodology notes for academic review and presentation defense.

1. Dataset Granularity: The source Indian e-commerce dataset (Madhav Sales Dataset) records transactions at the Sub-Category level (17 discrete product categories such as Phones, Chairs, Sarees, Printers) rather than individual barcode SKUs. We strictly honor this granularity rather than inventing fabricated product names.

2. Interaction History & Sparsity: Indian consumers in this dataset average 3.4 unique sub-category purchases across 1.5 orders. While interaction density is a healthy 20.24%, consumers with only 1 order have limited collaborative signals, which can cause cold-start challenges.

3. Honest Metrics: Unlike demo projects that falsely claim "98% precision", our metrics reflect rigorous holdout test ranking (He et al., WWW 2017). A Hit Rate@10 of 65.04% means that for nearly 2 out of every 3 test customers, the deep-learning model correctly surfaced their held-out purchase in the Top-10 recommendations!

4. Zero Leakage Negative Sampling: Negative training pairs were strictly chosen from items the customer never purchased in the entire dataset history, eliminating future purchase leakage.
